Ludo – free 4-player Ludo King style board game

About and how to play

Free online Ludo for 2 to 4 players

Play Ludo in your browser on a phone, iPad or laptop. Choose Human or Bot for each color, name the human players, and play pass-and-play on one device or against the computer. No download, no sign-up.

Ludo King style rules

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the yard.
  • A 6, a capture, or getting a token home gives you another roll.
  • Three 6s in a row lose your turn.
  • Stars and start squares are safe squares.
  • You need an exact roll to reach home.
  • The first player to bring all 4 tokens home wins.
